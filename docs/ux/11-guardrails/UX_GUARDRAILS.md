# UX Guardrails

## Purpose

Define the non-negotiable UX guardrails for AJ Digital OS.

## Guardrails

- No action without tenant context.
- No hidden AI automation for client-impacting workflows.
- No irreversible AI action without approval.
- No spinner-only AI loading states.
- No generic AI status copy.
- No dashboard with unprioritized metrics.
- No screen without empty, loading, error, and permission states.
- No cross-tenant data visibility.
- No role boundary ambiguity.
- No raw technical errors for non-technical users.
- No client-facing screen exposing internal orchestration complexity.
- No AI-generated recommendation without rationale or audit trail access.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The guardrails are clear enough for review and implementation.
