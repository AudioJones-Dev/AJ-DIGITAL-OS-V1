# Product Experience Principles

## Purpose

Define the product-level UX principles for AJ Digital OS.

## Principles

1. Control over complexity.
2. Business outcome visibility.
3. Tenant context clarity.
4. Approval safety.
5. Auditability by design.
6. Role-based access as UX.
7. Non-technical usability.
8. Fast comprehension.
9. AI transparency as trust infrastructure.
10. Accessibility as architecture.

## Doctrine

Each interface MUST expose the minimum context required to act safely.

Each interface SHOULD reduce orchestration noise and preserve the operator's sense of control.

Each interface MAY hide technical plumbing, but it MUST NOT hide approval state, tenant scope, or action risk.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The principle set is stable enough to guide screen specs.
- No principle conflicts with tenant isolation, approval, or accessibility doctrine.
- The principles can be translated into acceptance criteria for screens and flows.
