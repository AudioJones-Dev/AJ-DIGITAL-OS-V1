# Knowledge: AI Amplifies Question Quality

## Stable Understanding

AI is most useful when it improves the quality of questions, distinctions, and inquiry before producing answers or automation.

## Scope

Business Memory, founder strategy, diagnostic work, and AI-assisted decision support.

## Evidence Base

This is a working pilot note derived from Audio's approved first-note list. It needs examples from real use before promotion.

## Retrieval Use

Retrieve this note when evaluating whether an AI workflow is clarifying the problem or merely generating output.

## Known Limits

This note does not claim AI always improves reasoning. It depends on source quality, prompt discipline, retrieval context, and human review.

## Related Decisions

- COS is internal-only during the pilot.

## Review Notes

Use during the 30-day pilot to test whether Business Memory improves inquiry quality, not just answer speed.
