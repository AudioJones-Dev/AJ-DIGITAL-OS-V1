# Principle: Reality Is The Root; Operational Reality Is AJ Digital's Domain

## Principle

Reality is the root concept. Operational Reality is the AJ Digital scoped domain.

## Meaning

The system starts from what is actually happening. AJ Digital focuses that broader reality into the operating conditions, signals, decisions, systems, and outcomes that shape business performance.

## Evidence Base

This is a working pilot principle from Audio's first-note list and the approved Business Memory lifecycle.

## Scope

Business Memory, COS retrieval, doctrine testing, diagnostics, and future operational intelligence work.

## Failure Mode

If Operational Reality is treated as the root instead of the scoped domain, AJ Digital may collapse general truth-seeking into business operations too early.

None recorded yet.
